Questions

Frequently asked questions

How quickly will I receive my voucher?

Vouchers are delivered instantly to your VodaPay app and via SMS as soon as your payment is confirmed.

Can I buy a voucher for someone else?

Yes. Choose the "send as gift" option at checkout and enter the recipient's mobile number.

What payment methods are supported?

You can pay using your VodaPay wallet balance, linked debit/credit card, or instant EFT.

What happens if a voucher fails to deliver?

Any failed transaction is automatically reversed to your wallet within minutes, and you'll get a notification.

Is there a limit on how many vouchers I can buy?

Daily limits apply for security and are shown in-app before checkout, based on your account verification level.
